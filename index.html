Research Group

Students interested in these research directions can find more information on the Join the Group page.

Our research group is in the Department of Mathematical Data Science at Hanyang University ERICA in Ansan, South Korea. Our main goal is to understand and improve artificial intelligence.

Please refer to the research areas below for our current research interests.

Research Areas

Language Models

We investigate how language models learn, represent, and use linguistic information. We are interested in improving their learning methods and understanding the mechanisms behind their capabilities and limitations.

Vision-Language Models

We study models that jointly learn from visual and linguistic information. Our interests include multimodal learning, vision-language understanding, and learning from large-scale multimodal data.

Machine Learning and Deep Learning

Beyond our primary focus, we are interested in a broad range of topics in machine learning and deep learning. We welcome research questions that are fundamentally interesting, empirically grounded, and capable of expanding our understanding of AI models.
