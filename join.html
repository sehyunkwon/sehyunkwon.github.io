Join the Group

Our group is looking for motivated students interested in language models, vision-language models, or machine learning and deep learning.

Is This Group a Good Fit for You?

You may be a good fit if you:

  • Want to lead a research project and submit a first-author paper.

  • Want to apply your mathematical thinking to AI research.

  • Are highly motivated to grow rapidly as an AI researcher or AI engineer.

Possible Research Directions

Topics may include, but are not limited to:

  • Language models

  • Vision-language models

  • Retrieval, clustering, and classification

  • Other fundamental or applied problems in AI

Students are encouraged to bring their own research questions and ideas as well.

Getting in Touch

Please contact me at sehyunk [at] hanyang [dot] ac [dot] kr. In your email, please include:

  • A brief introduction

  • A short description of your research interests

  • Your academic transcript

  • (Optional) Any relevant research, project, or implementation experience
